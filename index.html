SMM services, Store delivery, payments, and order tracking.

Do you need my password?

The selected service explains the public target it needs, such as a profile, post, video, channel, or group link. Ethio Viral does not need your social account password.

What is in the catalog?

The catalog includes mobile recharge, Ethio Telecom data bundles, gaming credit, gift cards, streaming and subscription products, eSIM products, and other digital goods.

How do I track my orders?

Open the order details page. It keeps the submitted recipient, product or service name, amount, quantity, status, and available delivery information together.

What about delivery codes?

When a code is available, it can be revealed from the order or through a protected Ethio Viral delivery link. The recipient can view and copy it without seeing the upstream provider.

Which currency is used?

Wallet value is stored in USD. Deposit and customer prices can be displayed in ETB using the server exchange rate, and the saved order keeps the charge used at checkout.

How are orders updated?

The same order is checked for provider updates. Pending and processing orders remain visible until they complete or move to a final failed or refunded state.

Do you have an API?

Yes. The SMM V2 API supports service listing, order placement, status, balance, and refill workflows for agencies and child panels.
